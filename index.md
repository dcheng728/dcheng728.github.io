# About me

- I’m Chang Cheng (成畅), a physics MSc student at Imperial College London. I hold a BA in mathematics and computer science from Colorado College, USA, where a growing passion for physics during my final year led me to self-study the subject and ultimately pursue graduate studies at Imperial.
- My research interests lie in theoretical physics, particularly in quantum gravity, quantum field theory, black holes, and string theory.
- I’m currently writing my master’s dissertation on a possible 12-dimensional interpretation of the type IIB string theory. A summary of progress so far can be found in my literature review [(arXiv:2512.10746 [hep-th])](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10746).
