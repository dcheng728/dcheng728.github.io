# About me

- I’m Chang Cheng (成畅), a physics MSc student at Imperial College London.
I hold a BA in mathematics and CS from Colorado College, USA, where a growing passion for physics during my final year led me to self-study the subject and ultimately pursue physics at Imperial.
- My current interests are broadly in fundamental physics, particularly particle phenomenology, beyond Standard Model physics, quantum field theory, and quantum gravity.
- I’m currently writing my master’s dissertation on a possible 12-dimensional interpretation of the type IIB string theory. A summary of progress so far can be found in my literature review [(arXiv:2512.10746 [hep-th])](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.10746).
